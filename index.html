Our 4-Step Adoption Journey

We ensure our animals and adoptive families are a joyful, lifelong match through a friendly, guided process.

01

Find Your Match

Browse our verified profiles. Pick the puppy or kitten whose energy and temperament fits your home lifestyle.

02

WhatsApp Chat

Click the WhatsApp button on your chosen pet. Our caretakers will answer questions and share recent video clips.

03

Meet & Greet Visit

Visit our open green facility near Rajanukunte. Spend time playing and bonding with the pet in person.

04

Welcome Home

Complete simple paperwork, receive their vaccination record, and take home your new family member!
