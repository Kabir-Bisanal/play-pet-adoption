# Play Pet Adoption — Client Discovery Checklist & Questions

This checklist is designed for your discovery conversations with the Play Pet Boarding team over the next 24 to 48 hours.

---

## 1. Questions for the Client

### A. Branding & Domain
1. **Domain Name Preference:**
   - Do they want to register a new domain (e.g., `playpetadoption.in` / `playpetadoption.com`)?
   - Or would they like a free subdomain connected to their main site (e.g., `adopt.playpetboarding.com`)?
2. **Branding Assets:**
   - Can they provide the Play Pet Boarding logo (SVG or high-res PNG)?
   - Do they want to keep the same brand colors/fonts as `playpetboarding.com`, or should the adoption site have a slightly softer, warmer rescue-focused aesthetic?

### B. Pet Data & Media (~25 Animals)
1. **Pet Info Breakdown:**
   - Are the ~25 animals mostly dogs, mostly cats, or a balanced split?
   - Do they have high-resolution photos ready? (Ideally 2–4 photos per pet: portrait face shot, full body, playing/social shot).
   - Are there any video clips (Instagram reels or phone recordings)? Even a 10-second video of an animal wagging its tail or playing dramatically boosts adoption rates.
2. **Medical & Background Details:**
   - Are all 25 pets already vaccinated and dewormed?
   - Are they spayed/neutered?
   - Do any pets have special medical or dietary needs (e.g., senior dogs on joint supplements, special food)?

### C. Inquiry & Adoption Screening Workflow
1. **Preferred Communication Channel:**
   - In Bangalore, most pet seekers prefer instant WhatsApp messaging. Which WhatsApp number should receive adoption inquiries?
   - Would they like a direct WhatsApp button on each pet's profile, or do they want potential adopters to first fill out a detailed online application form? *(Recommendation: Offer both! A quick WhatsApp chat button for questions + a 5-minute pre-screening application).*
2. **Adoption Policy & Criteria:**
   - What are their basic requirements for an adopter? (e.g., must live in Bangalore, home visit required, apartment permission if renting).
   - Is there an adoption fee or voluntary medical care donation?

### D. Ongoing Updates & Team Management
1. **Who will update the pet statuses?**
   - Once a dog is adopted, does someone on their staff want to log in and mark it "Adopted", or will they prefer you to handle listing updates as part of a monthly maintenance plan?

## 2. Pet Data Collection Template (Shareable with Client)

To make it effortless for the client to provide details for the 25 pets, share this simple table or create a Google Sheet with these columns:

| Column Name | Description | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pet Name** | Name of the dog or cat | *Bruno* |
| **Species** | Dog / Cat | *Dog* |
| **Breed / Mix** | Breed or mix | *Indie (Indian Pariah Dog)* |
| **Gender** | Male / Female | *Male* |
| **Approximate Age** | Age in months or years | *1.5 Years* |
| **Size** | Small / Medium / Large | *Medium (~18 kg)* |
| **Health Status** | Vaccinated / Spayed / Dewormed | *Fully vaccinated, neutered, dewormed* |
| **Special Needs?** | Any medical or dietary needs | *None, healthy and energetic* |
| **Personality / Quirks** | 2-3 sentences on temperament | *Loves belly rubs, very friendly with other dogs, leash trained, great with older kids.* |
| **Backstory** | How they joined Play Pet Boarding | *Rescued near Rajanukunte, fostered and rehabilitated at our facility.* |
| **Photos Folder Link** | Google Drive / Dropbox link with photos | *Link to folder: `Bruno_Photos/`* |
| **Current Status** | Available / Adoption Pending / Adopted | *Available* |
